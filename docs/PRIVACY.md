# KIN — Privacy & Safety

KIN touches identity, location and potential family relationships, so privacy is part of the interaction model.

## Prototype rules

- Fictional people
- Synthetic relationship signals
- Synthetic locations
- No real DNA provider
- No real kinship verification

## Principles

### Approximate location

A nearby signal should not expose another person's exact location.

### Anonymous by default

A possible match should not immediately reveal name, photo or profile.

### Mutual reveal

Identity should only become visible after both sides explicitly choose to connect.

### Clear uncertainty

Use “possible”, “may”, “confidence” and “estimated”. Never turn probabilistic inference into a claim of biological certainty.

### Data minimisation

A production service should retain only information needed for the feature being used.

## Future risk areas

Genetic data, minors, stalking, domestic abuse, location privacy, false relationship claims, impersonation, harassment, deletion and consent withdrawal all require dedicated product, legal and security work before real-world deployment.
