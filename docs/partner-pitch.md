## Explore the concept

KIN explores a more visual and engaging way to discover personal origins, family history and the stories that connect places and generations.

The product direction is built around:
- **Visual Origin Discovery:** My Origins, Origins Map and Migration Stories.
- **DNA Discovery:** an optional future enrichment path, dependent on a suitable partner and explicit user consent.
- **Social Connection:** KIN Radar as a secondary, consent-led discovery experience.

## Current prototype

[Open the KIN demo](https://id-preview--3f3b6825-eb35-41bb-87e2-874a0e652f85.lovable.app)

The current demo is an early, frontend-only prototype. It uses local browser storage and demo/user-entered content. It does not provide production accounts, cloud persistence, live DNA analysis, verified genealogy, live matching or partner integrations.

## Partnership opportunity

We welcome exploratory conversations with organisations that may help validate, develop or complement the KIN experience. The immediate next step is a discovery conversation followed, if there is mutual interest, by a focused product and technical feasibility workshop.

Potential areas to explore include origin storytelling, data enrichment, DNA-related experiences and privacy-first social discovery. No partnership, API access, endorsement or integration is currently confirmed.
