# KIN

**Discover connections before identities.**

KIN is a social discovery concept exploring ancestry signals, approximate location, consent and social discovery.

## Status

**KIN v0.1 — public concept prototype.**

This repository documents the clickable MVP prototype built in Lovable. All people, locations and relationship signals in the demo are synthetic.

## Core loop

1. Discover a possible connection nearby.
2. Understand the possible relationship and confidence.
3. Keep identity hidden by default.
4. Mutually reveal identity.
5. Connect through lightweight messaging.

## Prototype

Lovable preview: https://id-preview--3f3b6825-eb35-41bb-87e2-874a0e652f85.lovable.app

## Product principle

**Discover connections before identities.**

KIN explores the space between ancestry products and social networks: ancestry tells you where you come from; social networks tell you who you know; KIN asks who you might be connected to right now.

## Important

KIN is a concept/prototype. It does not currently connect to a real DNA provider, ancestry database or live kinship verification service.
