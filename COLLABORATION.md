# Collaboration & intellectual property

**Future-by-Fie is an independent innovation studio.** This repository is a public concept demonstration intended to explain an idea, show early technical exploration and make it easier for potential collaborators to evaluate the opportunity.

## Status and permissions
The repository is public for viewing and evaluation. **No open-source license is currently granted.** Unless a file explicitly states otherwise, source code, documentation, designs, names and other original materials remain subject to their respective owners' rights. Public visibility does not mean permission to copy, redistribute, modify or commercially exploit the materials.

This is not a production-ready product, an offer of employment, or an invitation to submit unpaid development work. Do not submit pull requests, code, designs or other contributions unless a collaboration arrangement has first been agreed in writing.

## Interested in building something together?
We welcome thoughtful approaches from developers, potential technical co-founders, pilot customers and commercial partners who see a specific opportunity in this concept.

Visit **https://www.future-by-fie.com/#partners** and contact us through the website. Please mention the project, your relevant background and the opportunity you would like to explore.

Any collaboration, access to non-public material, licensing, ownership of new work, confidentiality, compensation or commercial participation would be discussed and agreed separately in writing before work begins.

## Important limitations
Concept descriptions and demos are exploratory. They do not establish technical feasibility, market demand, freedom to operate, or third-party rights clearance. Third-party dependencies and trademarks remain with their respective owners.
